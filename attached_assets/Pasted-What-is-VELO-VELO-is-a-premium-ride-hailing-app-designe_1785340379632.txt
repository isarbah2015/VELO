What is VELO?
VELO is a premium ride-hailing app designed exclusively for bike (motorcycle/okada) transportation in Ghana. Think of it as Uber for bikes — but built specifically for Ghanaian riders with local features, payments, and safety in mind.

The Problem We're Solving
Issue	How VELO Fixes It
Unsafe okada rides	Verified drivers, GPS tracking, emergency SOS
Unfair pricing	Transparent fare calculator (base + per km + per min)
Cash-only payments	Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo) + card
No driver accountability	Rating system, driver profiles, Ghana Card verification
Hard to find bikes	Live map showing nearby bikers with ETA
No ride history	Complete ride history with receipts
Safety concerns	Share ride with family, emergency contacts, real-time tracking
Who Uses VELO?
1. Riders (Passengers)
People in Accra, Kumasi, Tema, and other Ghanaian cities

Need fast, affordable transportation

Want safety and transparency

Prefer cashless payments (MoMo)

2. Bikers (Drivers)
Motorcycle/okada drivers

Want more rides and fair earnings

Need GPS navigation and ride management

Want to build a reputation with ratings

Why "VELO"?
Velo comes from:

Velocity - Speed and fast transportation

Velocipede - Early bicycle/motorcycle term

"Velo" - Means "I see" in Latin (trust and visibility)
